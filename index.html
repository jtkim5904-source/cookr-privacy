1. Introduction

Cook-A-Noodle-Do! ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by an individual developer (JT Kim). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use the Cook-A-Noodle-Do! mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address and password used to create and manage your account (processed via Supabase Auth).
  • Ingredients: Names and descriptions of ingredients you manually enter through the App.
  • Saved recipes: Recipe data you choose to bookmark or save within the App.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Search history: Queries you enter in the recipe search function are stored locally on your device only and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Device information: Basic device type and operating system version may be collected for debugging purposes.

2.3 Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each acts as a data processor under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA):

  • OpenAI, Inc. (USA): Recipe search text is sent to OpenAI to generate recipe recommendations. (Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase, Inc. (USA): Account credentials, saved ingredients, and recipe data are stored on Supabase servers. Supabase maintains a Data Processing Agreement available at supabase.com/legal/dpa. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google LLC / YouTube (USA): Recipe-related video searches are performed via the YouTube Data API. (Privacy Policy)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Generate and display personalized recipe recommendations
  • Store and retrieve your saved ingredients and recipes
  • Display relevant recipe videos
  • Improve App performance and features
  • Respond to support inquiries

We do not use your information for advertising, sell or share your data to third parties, or use it beyond what is stated in this policy.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Data processors: Third parties listed in Section 2.3, operating under DPAs and their own privacy policies
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public

6. Data Security

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring users can only access their own data
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Account Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data directly within the App via Settings → Delete Account. Alternatively, you may contact us at jtkim5904@gmail.com and we will permanently delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete such information.

1. 개요

Cook-A-Noodle-Do!(이하 "서비스")는 개인 개발자(김준태)가 운영합니다. 본 개인정보처리방침은 Cook-A-Noodle-Do! 앱 이용 시 수집되는 개인정보의 처리 방법을 설명하며, 「개인정보 보호법」(PIPA)에 따라 작성되었습니다.

2. 수집하는 개인정보 항목 및 방법

2.1 이용자가 직접 제공하는 정보

  • 계정 정보: 회원 가입 및 로그인에 사용되는 이메일 주소 및 비밀번호 (Supabase Auth를 통해 처리)
  • 재료 정보: 앱에 직접 입력한 재료 이름 및 설명
  • 저장된 레시피: 앱 내에서 북마크하거나 저장한 레시피 데이터

2.2 서비스 이용 중 자동 수집되는 정보

  • 검색 기록: 레시피 검색 기능에서 입력한 검색어는 이용자의 기기에만 로컬로 저장되며, 당사 서버로 전송되지 않습니다.
  • 기기 정보: 디버깅 목적으로 기기 종류 및 운영체제 버전이 수집될 수 있습니다.

2.3 개인정보 처리 위탁

서비스 운영을 위해 다음 제3자에게 개인정보 처리를 위탁합니다:

  • OpenAI, Inc. (미국): 레시피 검색 텍스트가 레시피 추천 생성을 위해 전송됩니다. (개인정보처리방침)
  • Supabase, Inc. (미국): 계정 정보, 저장된 재료, 레시피 데이터가 저장됩니다. Supabase의 데이터 처리 계약(DPA)은 supabase.com/legal/dpa에서 확인할 수 있습니다. (개인정보처리방침)
  • Google LLC / YouTube (미국): 레시피 관련 영상 검색을 위해 YouTube Data API가 사용됩니다. (개인정보처리방침)

위 위탁처들은 미국에 서버를 두고 있으며, 이용자의 개인정보는 해당 서버로 이전됩니다. 이에 동의하지 않을 경우 앱 이용이 제한될 수 있습니다.
